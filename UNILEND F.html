Bio

UNILEND is a peer-to-peer web platform built to make campus life more affordable, collaborative, and connected. By providing a safe, verified marketplace for students to list, lend, or transfer textbooks, lab equipment, and essential tech, Unilend removes the financial strain of academic materials.
